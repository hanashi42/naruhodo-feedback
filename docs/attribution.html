Naruhodo — Data Sources and Licences

Last updated: October 24, 2026 · Privacy Policy

Naruhodo bundles the following third-party data. Everything below runs locally inside the extension; nothing is fetched from these projects while you read.

JMdict, JMnedict and KANJIDIC2

This package uses the JMdict, JMnedict and KANJIDIC2 dictionary files. These files are the property of the Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group (EDRDG), and are used in conformance with the Group's licence: https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html — the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike Licence 4.0 (CC BY-SA 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

  • JMdict / JMnedict: https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project
  • KANJIDIC2: https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/KANJIDIC_Project
  • The files reach Naruhodo as the JSON conversions published by jmdict-simplified (https://github.com/scriptin/jmdict-simplified), distributed under the same licence.

KanjiVG (kanji parts)

The 部件 (parts) row on the kanji card is derived from KanjiVG (https://kanjivg.tagaini.net), copyright (C) 2009-2013 Ulrich Apel, licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 licence (CC BY-SA 3.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). Naruhodo keeps only the first layer of each character's structure (語 = 言 + 吾) and how its radical is written; that derived table, dict/kanji-parts.json, is distributed under the same licence. The radical number itself comes from KANJIDIC2 (above).
